## Phase 1: Discovery & Strategy

### The Product "Hook"
### The Product "Hook"
Qook Commander is an **intelligent kitchen operating system**, built for the complexity of Indian homes but perfect for everyone.
*   **The Angle**: "If it can manage a 4-dish Indian dinner + Tiffin for tomorrow, it can handle your Pasta Night in its sleep."
*   **Core Problem**: "What's for dinner?" + "complex prep logic" (soaking/marinating) + "random ingredients."
*   **The Vibe**: Premium, Intelligent, Deeply Capable. "The Chef that knows your heritage and your schedule."

### Competitor Insights (What worked for others)
*   **PlanEat AI**: 7 months of prep (we might need a compressed version), heavy community focus.
*   **OH, a potato!**: "Zero Waste" angle was huge. We should highlight the "Fridge Photo" feature as our zero-waste angle.
*   **Ultimate Meal Plans**: "Save ₹4,000 ($50) / week and 2 hours." specific numbers sell. We need to measure or estimate our user's savings.

### Target Audience
### Target Audience
*   **Primary (The "Power Planner"):** Indian diaspora & Desi households managing complex daily meals (Tiffin + Dinner).
*   **Secondary (Global):** Busy professionals/families who want "Meal Prep" features (the Tiffin feature re-branded as "Lunch Prep").
*   **Tech-forward home cooks** who want a "smart kitchen" setup.
