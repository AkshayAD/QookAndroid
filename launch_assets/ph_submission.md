## 4. The Maker's Comment (First Comment)

Hi Product Hunt! 👋 I’m Akshay, maker of Qook Commander.

**The Problem:** Most meal planners assume you cook one dish. But in my home (and millions of Indian households), a "meal" is a complex orchestration: Roti + Dal + Sabzi + something for tomorrow's Tiffin (Lunchbox).

I’d buy ₹8,500 ($100) of groceries... and then order takeout because the **mental load** of planning this orchestration was too high. My fridge became a graveyard of good intentions (and rotting spinach).

**The Solution:** Qook Commander. An AI OS for your kitchen that understands **Complexity**.

We built it to handle the hardest use case (Indian cooking), which makes it a superpower for *any* cuisine:
1.  **"Prep is half the battle"**: The app knows you need to "Soak Rajma" tonight for tomorrow's lunch. (Global appeal: Marinating steaks, rising dough).
2.  **"Tiffin Logic"**: It plans dinner *and* your packed lunch for the next day automatically.
3.  **"The Fridge Photo"**: Have random veggies? Snap a photo. Our AI suggests recipes that use them up (Zero Waste).

**For the Global Community:** We found that by solving for the "Power User" (complex cultural meals), we built a more robust planner for everyone. Whether you're meal prepping Burrito Bowls or a full Thali, Qook has you covered.

Let me know what you think! 👇
