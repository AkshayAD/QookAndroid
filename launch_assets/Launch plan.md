When to start preparing for the Product Hunt launch?

When we interviewed, 80% of the successful makers said that they started preparing for their launch 1 month ahead. So it’s highly recommended to start your pre-launch preparation a month back. But if you can’t find time, you need to start preparing at least 2 weeks before.

Stage 1 - Pre launch1.1

Finalizing the launch day

There are 2 types of launch days on product hunt:

High traffic days:

Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday

Since a lot of people visit Product Hunt on these days, you will have huge competition.

Low traffic days:

Weekends, Monday and Friday

As not many people visit on these days, you won’t have that much competition.Depending on your launch goals and your audience sizes, finalize a luanch day.

1.2 Finding the Hunter

Hunter is the person who launches a product on Product Hunt. You can launchyour own product or get a hunter who’s popular in your niche to launch your product.Having a hunter helps you:

Build authority to your product

Drive significant traffic to your launch

Help with Strategizing the launch and Optimizing the listing

How to Find Hunters?

– Go to phhunters.com (A list of top hunters on product hunt)

– Filter your product category

– Reach out to hunters who are popular in your niche

1.3 Schedule the launch and create a teaser page

Product Hunt gives you a teaser page to promote your launch once it’s scheduled. You can share the teaser page link with your friends/followers and ask them to click the “notify me” on this page to receive a notification when your launch goes live.

How to get teaser page link:

Once you schedule your launch, you will see an option called “Create Teaser” next to your product

Click on it and add– Title– Description and– A banner image

Then, click on submit.

Now, the Product Hunt team will verify and take your teaser page live.Once the teaser page is created, it’s time to build audience for your launch. Your goal is to get as many supporters as you can for the launch.

1.4 Promote it on social media

Share your teaser page and ask your followers to click notify me button.

Post ideas:

- Create content around your product and plug your launch-

Create memes around your product, problem and customer journey, and plug your product launch in the post.

Share updates on your product’s progress with followers and keep reminding them about your launch

Give away a free product on Twitter/Linkedin or launch a side project on Product Hunt. This will help in building an audience before the main launch.

1.5 Reachouts

Reach out to your maker friends, personal network, and your supporters on social media and notify them that you need their support when you launch on product hunt.

1.6 Send Emails

Send emails to your customers and notify them about your launch. Ask them to support your launch by upvoting your launch or giving feedback in the comments. You can use these [3 email templates](https://launchpedia.gumroad.com/l/email-ideas-for-ph-launch) to get support for your product hunt launch.

1.7 Be active on Product Hunt discussions

Post regularly on product hunt discussions about your launch, and ask questions that will resonate with your target audience. This way, you can get a list of your product’s target audience who can your support on the launch day.

1.8 Prepare assets

In addition to the title, There are 6 content assets you should add to your product hunt listing page.

Tagline: A one line description about your product

Description: Share more information about your product and what it does in 260 charcacters

Thumbnail: You can use your brand logo or a GIF that relates your audience and their pain points, as your thumbnail.

Images: It is recommended to upload at least 4 or more images to get featured on Product Hunt.

Videos: Add a product video to demonstrate your product to the visitors in a live and engaging way.

Maker comment : Maker comment is the first comment on your launch page. Use it to share your story with the visitors and the reason behind building your product.

Stage 2- Launch Day

First and foremost, you should block the entire day for Product Hunt launch.Don’t have any distractions or commitments on the launch day. You have to give 100% of the time to your launch on launch day.

2.1 The first 4 hours

First 4 hours of a Product Hunt launch are make-or-break! During this time, Your launch will be shown randomly to the site visitor, so go all-in on promotion and upvotes.

After the initial 4 hours, rankings will be given based on upvotes.

So, you should completely focus on promoting your launch & getting the maximum upvotes in these first 4 hours.

2.2 Reaching out

Once the launch goes live, Reach out to

Maker friends

Your ex-colleagues and

Competitor audience

Make sure that you message them before the launch day &check whether they are ready to support you or not. Do not spam them.

2.3 Post on social media and communities

Post about your launch on

Twitter

Linkedin

Indiehackers.com

Product Hunt Discussions

2.4 Send Emails

Send emails to your subscribers & ask support for your launch. It’s better to segment your audience based on the time zones for sending broadcasts.

2.5 Reply on the launch page

Engaging with the comments & reviews left by people on your launch page is important. If you and another product share the same number of upvotes on launch day, the one that has more engagement will rank higher. So, reply to each and every comment on Product Hunt.

2.6 Post Updates on Social Media

Post hourly updates about your launch on Twitter and Linkedin

You can post:

Number of upvotes and comments

Reviews you received on the launch day

Upvotes milestones and

Rank updates

Stage 3: Post launch

3.1 Thank your Supporters

Once the launch is done, send thank you messages to your

Email subscribers

Social media followers and

Maker friends

Add the Product Hunt page link in the thank you message, so that if anyone hasn’t upvoted on the launch day, they can still upvote it. This will be helpful for Product of the week and month rankings.

3.2 Launch on other platforms

Your website traffic will start dropping once your product hunt launch is over.So Once the Product Hunt launch is completed, launch on platforms like Betalist, Startups.fyi, insanely cool tools, uneed, dev hunt, fazier, etc
